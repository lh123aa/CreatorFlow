# 街头潮流涂鸦

## 适用内容
- 街头潮流、嘻哈文化
- 品牌态度、青年文化
- 音乐、街头艺术
- 硬核不羁、真实表达

## 背景提示词
```
Street trend graffiti style, cream-yellow paper background with rough texture, multiple layers of black crayon scribbles and red-blue color blocks, natural frayed edges, young male character in center with curly black hair, arm covering half face, eye tattoo on forearm with "N" letter, wearing white T-shirt with dot pattern, silver chain necklace, "HIP HOP" black graffiti text with crown, "BEATS" "YO!" "STAY REAL" text, vintage radio drawing, scattered composition, energetic hardcore hip-hop atmosphere
```

## 文字配色方案
| 用途 | 颜色 | 色值 |
|------|------|------|
| 背景 | 米黄纸 | #FEF3C7 |
| 涂鸦线 | 黑色蜡笔 | #111827 |
| 色块 | 红蓝黄 | 多色 |
| 人物 | 黑白灰 | 多色 |
| 皇冠 | 红/黄 | #DC2626/#FBBF24 |

## 装饰元素建议
- 黑色蜡笔乱线
- 红蓝色块涂鸦
- 手绘皇冠
- 复古收录机
- 银色链条项链

## 注意事项
- 散点式构图
- 撞色与动感线条
- 粗糙肌理增加街头感
- 传递真实不羁的嘻哈精神
