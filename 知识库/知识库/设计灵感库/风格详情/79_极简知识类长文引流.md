# 极简知识类长文引流

## 适用内容
- 知识分享、长文引流
- 方法论、系统化内容
- 专业干货、个人IP
- 阅读引导、内容预告

## 背景提示词
```
Minimalist knowledge poster style, pure cream-white background with sparse light cream irregular dots creating subtle texture, no layer stacking, hand-drawn double quotation marks as visual guide at top, bold dark brown sans-serif title in three lines, small signature and reading time below with hand-drawn arrow, vertical centered layout, professional yet approachable atmosphere, suitable for long-form content teaser
```

## 文字配色方案
| 用途 | 颜色 | 色值 |
|------|------|------|
| 背景 | 米白 | #FFFBEB |
| 主标题 | 深棕褐 | #44403C |
| 署名 | 浅棕褐 | #78716C |
| 装饰 | 浅米色手绘 | #FEF3C7 |

## 装饰元素建议
- 手写双引号
- 手绘箭头
- 极简肌理斑点

## 注意事项
- 垂直居中排版
- 标题占视觉重心
- 低饱和棕褐色系
- 专业严谨兼具亲和力
- 适合长文引流海报
