# 手绘松弛治愈风

## 适用内容
- 治愈内容分享
- 邀请函、婚礼请柬
- 温馨活动通知
- 生活记录、心情随笔

## 背景提示词
```
Hand-drawn relaxed healing style collage, low saturation sage green background, torn paper edge with blue-white grid paper underneath, hand-drawn black and white line art (cat at dining table, wine bottles, strawberries, roses, heart ornaments), soft and warm atmosphere, vertical double-column symmetrical layout, handwritten style typography, starburst decorations, bow tie line drawings, pastel color palette blocks (olive green, off-white, soft pink, bright white, pure black), vintage photo frame with avatar, gentle and inviting mood, cozy life aesthetic
```

## 文字配色方案
| 用途 | 颜色 | 色值 |
|------|------|------|
| 主标题 | 深灰棕 | #5C5C5C |
| 手写文字 | 深黑 | #1F2937 |
| 装饰 | 浅灰 | #9CA3AF |
| 背景 | 灰绿 | #E8EDE6 |
| 网格纸层 | 蓝白格 | #F0F4F8 |
| 配色块 | 橄榄绿 | #708238 |

## 装饰元素建议
- 猫咪餐桌线稿
- 酒杯、婚礼蛋糕小元素
- 蝴蝶结线稿
- 星芒装饰
- 配色方块展示
- 圆形头像框
- 纸飞机图标
- 花瓶、酒杯手绘

## 注意事项
- 竖向双栏对称式构图
- 大量手绘线稿增加温度
- 配色方块提供视觉层次
- 保持轻松治愈的氛围
- 手写字体传递亲切感
