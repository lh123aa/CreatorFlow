# 超现实仪式感摄影

## 适用内容
- 概念表达、艺术创作
- 哲学思考、深度内容
- 品牌形象片、艺术海报
- 严肃主题的视觉表达

## 背景提示词
```
Surrealist ritual photography style, realistic photo quality, eye-level shot, medium composition, pure bright blue cloudless sky occupying two-thirds of frame, light gray-white paved walkway, sharp bright lighting, clean and crisp color palette, solemn yet absurd atmosphere, adult male figure in indigo blue wide-sleeved robe reaching to ground, exaggerated white wide-brimmed hat with upward curving rabbit-ear shaped structures, white fabric covering neck and partial face, holding bright orange rectangular book, walking toward viewer, followed by three figures in matching beige robes with smaller rabbit-ear hats, walking in ceremonial procession, minimalist surreal aesthetic
```

## 装饰元素建议
- 极简构图，少即是多
- 夸张的服饰道具（兔耳帽）
- 仪式感的队列排列
- 纯净的色彩块面
- 严肃与荒诞的反差

## 注意事项
- 平视镜头，中景构图
- 光影要明亮锐利
- 色调干净明快
- 保持肃穆又荒诞的张力
- 文字叠加要克制，不破坏画面
- 这种风格适合留白，让画面自己说话
