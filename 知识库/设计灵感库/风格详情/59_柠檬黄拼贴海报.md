# 柠檬黄拼贴海报

## 适用内容
- 展览海报、艺术活动
- 音乐节、文化节
- 设计展览、艺术沙龙
- 活力四射、年轻潮流

```
Lemon yellow collage poster collection style, 3x3 grid layout on black background, high saturation lemon yellow as core color, strong contrast with black, dark blue, gray, each poster with clean cut edges and distinct theme,书法字体 mixed with Japanese and English titles, geometric shapes, abstract elements, layered visual stacking, energetic and vibrant atmosphere, modern experimental design
```

## 装饰元素建议
- 书法字体
- 日文标题
- 抽象几何图形
- 展览信息
- 闪电、星星等符号

## 注意事项
- 九宫格规整排列
- 柠檬黄与黑形成强烈撞色
- 每张海报独立风格但整体统一
- 层叠的视觉秩序感
