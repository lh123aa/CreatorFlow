# 传统年画武财神

## 适用内容
- 传统年画、财神吉祥
- 新春祝福、节日海报
- 国潮品牌、中式美学
- 招财纳福、吉祥寓意

## 背景提示词
```
Traditional Chinese New Year painting style, indigo blue background with mottled texture and subtle dark patterns, aged vintage edges, martial wealth god Zhao Gongming sitting on black tiger, fierce expression with thick beard, armor with fish-scale and scroll patterns in cream-white and indigo, tiger in pouncing pose, red seal stamps at corners, gold and indigo calligraphy text, symmetrical layout, solemn and majestic atmosphere, auspicious blessing meaning
```
