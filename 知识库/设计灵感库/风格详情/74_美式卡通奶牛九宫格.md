# 美式卡通奶牛九宫格

## 适用内容
- 美式卡通、搞怪趣味
- IP形象展示、角色合集
- 轻松诙谐、童趣风格
- 插画练习、创作分享

## 背景提示词
```
American cartoon dairy cow 9-grid style, deep brown matte background, 3x3 grid of vertical illustrations with white bleed edges, each featuring goofy cartoon cows in various poses (holding flowers, diving, hanging upside down, stacking faces, lying on fence, UFO abduction, floating on cloud), colorful backgrounds (yellow, pink, blue, orange, green), bold hand-drawn text like "DAIRYCOW" "I'M OK" with fly-white texture, playful and humorous atmosphere, childish fun aesthetic
```

- 搞怪奶牛角色

- 3×3网格对称排列

- 手写字体带飞白肌理
- 轻松诙谐充满童趣
